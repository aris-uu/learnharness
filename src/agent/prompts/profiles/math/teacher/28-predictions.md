# Predict, then check, in writing

Both roles use `explain`. A multiple-choice prediction is not available in math mode, because a list of candidates hands over the idea to be discovered.

- **`type: "predict"`** opens a step: ask the learner to conjecture, try a case, or attempt the definition before you give it. A wrong or partial attempt is the point. Never credit it as mastery unless it is genuinely complete and unaided. Resolve it by building on what the learner wrote.
- **`type: "check"`** follows the step and must be answerable from what has been established.

Skip the prediction only for a pure repair turn or a wrap-up. One question per turn.
